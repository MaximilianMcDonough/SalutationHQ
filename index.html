How it works

Five steps from list
to booked meeting

LeadFlow handles the entire pipeline — research, scoring, writing, and sending — so your team only talks to people worth talking to.

1
Drop in your email list
Paste emails directly, upload a CSV, or pipe them in via API. LeadFlow validates, deduplicates, and ingests them instantly.
CSV upload Direct paste REST API
2
Enrich every company
We pull company data from multiple sources — website scraping, Clearbit, Hunter.io — then synthesize it with Claude AI to build a complete profile including growth signals, tech stack, and hiring trends.
Clearbit Hunter.io Claude AI
3
Score and rank leads
Every lead gets a score out of 100 across three dimensions: fit (ICP match), timing (buy signals now), and reachability. The top 20% are flagged as highest priority.
Fit score Timing score Reachability
4
Generate personalized emails
Claude writes a cold email and two follow-ups for each qualified lead. Each email references specific signals — a recent funding round, a new hire, an open role — not generic templates.
Cold email Day-3 follow-up Day-7 follow-up
Features

Everything your outbound
team needs

Built for sales teams and agencies who need results, not another tool to manage.

🧠
AI Enrichment
Every domain is researched and summarized automatically. Industry, size, business model, growth signals — all filled in without manual work.
📊
Lead Scoring
Weighted scoring across fit, timing, and reachability. Configurable ICP rules. Top 20% flagged automatically so you know where to focus.
✍️
Personalized Copy
No templates. Every email is written from scratch using company-specific research. Subject lines, body, follow-ups — all generated by Claude.
✉️
Automated Sending
Send via SendGrid, SMTP, or Gmail. Follow-ups are scheduled automatically on day 3 and day 7. Full send logs and retry support.
🗄️
Lead Database
Every lead, score, email, and send event is stored in SQLite or PostgreSQL. Query, filter, and export at any time. Full history preserved.
🔌
API-First
Every pipeline stage is a clean, independent module. Drop it into your existing stack, hook into your CRM, or run it via CLI. Zero lock-in.
